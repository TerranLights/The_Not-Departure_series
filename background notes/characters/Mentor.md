The Mentor [[unnamed, background/historical — likely deceased by the
story's present day]]

Not an on-page character in real time — a historical/backstory figure
referenced by the Resistance Contact, giving the audience a direct,
textured throughline back to the Long Night War and the Falkland Treaty
exile without requiring any currently-living character to implausibly have
witnessed it firsthand. No full character-arc sheet for this reason (see
../../structure/character arcs/, which only covers on-page cast).

Identity: an elder robot who personally lived through the Long Night War
(2812) and fled off-world during the evacuation via Amundsen Tower — one of
the original off-world evacuees. Mentored the Resistance Contact at some
point in her life.

Lifespan math: the original draft (Tepenian_Reinterpretation.md §3.5) placed
this character's death ~300-450 years post-war, comfortably before the
story's present, under the OLD 600-700-years-post-war dating. That dating
has since been superseded (series is now set 1-4 generations after
*The Cryptograph Helix*'s close — a much tighter gap) — this math needs
redoing. See ../../structure/Dev-Road-Map/02-Blocking-Decisions.md.
