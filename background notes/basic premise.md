A serialized audio drama (30+ episodes, radio-play style), reinterpreting the
unfinished 2013 ASMR series *Departure* (creator: Ally; two episodes, Part 3
never made) into the Tepenian universe — the same shared continuity as
*The Cryptograph Helix*, *Inner Tepenia*, and the *Outer Tepenia* series.
Title is still a placeholder; "Departure" and "The Not-Departure" are both
working labels only.

Set roughly one to four generations after the close of *The Cryptograph
Helix*, well before the opening of *Outer Tepenia 1* — the exact year is an
open blocking decision (see `../structure/Dev-Road-Map/02-Blocking-Decisions.md`),
since neither Cryptograph Helix's own closing date nor the Solar Colonization
era's bounds are pinned down yet in the master timeline repo.

Doc, a researcher who cracked full-continuity consciousness transfer between
living substrates, has the breakthrough taken and suppressed by Earthtech.
Betrayed and dying aboard a commercial exo-solar flight, Doc is contacted
mid-stasis by a resistance movement fighting Earthtech's monopoly on the
technology and its synthetic-biological servitor underclass — engineered
specifically to dodge the robot-personhood protections this universe's robots
already won centuries earlier.
