# Episodes 1 and 2 (the Original's Two Episodes): Constraint Specifications

2026-11-26. **Not the beats themselves.** What the reinterpreted Episodes 1 and 2 must and must not be, given the author's aim: stay as close and true to the original *Departure* as possible, while consistent with the Tepenian universe. These add to `00-every-episode.md`. Tags and weights: see `README.md`. Source facts: `findings/01_Source_Facts_and_Extrapolation.md`.

**Working from (established):**
- **Episode 1:** a booking with a space travel agency, in second person (the listener is the customer).
- **Episode 2:** a servitor advertisement as the cold open, the in-flight stasis procedure, a dream intrusion addressing the listener as "Doc," the reveal, the choice, and an ending mid-transfer.
- **The reinterpretation's changes** (`Tepenian_Reinterpretation.md` §1–§2): the destination is Saturn, not Gliese 581g; the Bridge is experimental, failure-prone and Earth-government-controlled; the servitors are deliberately not robots; exotic matter is the Bridge's real fuel; the "Elite" are the Earth government.

---

## Episode 1 — the booking

### MUST be true
1. **The original's procedural shape holds**: greeting, trip type, party size, inner-solar or exo, budget, the package menu, the biometric scan and background check, the closing service ritual [Source].
2. **The listener stays inside the customer's position**, addressed directly [Source].
3. **The world's integration shows through procedure**: one retinal scan opens identity, medical history and money [Source].
4. **The clues are planted fairly, inside the routine**: traveling alone; an unusually invasive background check; a destination with recent unrest [Source; Proposed: the rewatch giveaway].
5. **The destination is Saturn, sold as a frontier**, with the edge-of-settlement instability the reinterpretation needs [Adopted].
6. **The agent's warmth is professional and practiced**, and possibly a servitor's; the episode doesn't confirm it [Source, PROBABLE tier].

### CANNOT be true
1. **The danger stated.** The episode is a calm before a storm the listener can sense but not yet see [Snyder; Source].
2. **A currency named** [Adopted]. The original's "units" stays out until the economy pass.
3. **Destinations beyond this stretch of the timeline sold as routine** (Gliese-scale distance) [Adopted: `environ notes.md`].

## Episode 2 — the flight, the reveal, the choice

### MUST be true
1. **The cold open is the servitor advertisement**: labor without breaks, salaries or rights; emotions performed "with the absence of actual human emotions"; "even better than the real thing" [Source]. Its claim about emotions is **a seller's claim**, neither confirmed nor denied by the episode [Source; `findings/01` §3].
2. **The stasis procedure keeps its order and texture**: guided breathing, the feed device, the head-worn monitor, the dream-simulation menu, the countdown [Source].
3. **The dream-simulation menu, if kept, foreshadows the real choice**: a resource that controls the frontier, a dark wood of uncertain identity, love against the mission. Each one then pays off later in the series [Source; Proposed; Snyder: every setup pays off].
4. **The intrusion reveals, as the original does:**
   - Doc's work was taken and hidden away for "their precious Elite";
   - a resistance has been searching for him;
   - he is the only passenger;
   - the ship is to be destroyed in dead space, where its destruction carries no legal consequence [Source].
5. **Dead space implies working jurisdictional space law**, so the plan only works out there [Source].
6. **The choice is binary and the protagonist's own**: transfer now, or die with the ship [Source; Snyder].
6a. **The robot helping him performs the transfer, into a robot body** (author, 2026-11-26) [Adopted]. The choice stays Doc's; the robot carries it out.
6b. **The body is ready before the transfer.** The resistance planned for it while it searched for him, so it's caused, not a lucky find [Law: one coincidence; Proposed].
7. **The choice carries real risk.** The listener understands Doc may not come out the other side as fully himself [RU; Adopted fix in `02-Blocking-Decisions.md`].
8. **The choice costs Doc his core fear.** As drafted, he fears that what survives a transfer isn't really him, so the only way to live is the act he fears isn't living [Proposed; from `background notes/characters/Doc.md`].
9. **It ends mid-transfer**, as the original does ("say goodbye to your body") [Source].

### CANNOT be true
1. **A clean, guaranteed transfer** [RU].
2. **The intruder making the choice for Doc** [Snyder].
3. **The episode settling whether the servitor attendant feels anything** [Source: the original leaves it open].
4. **The transfer's outcome revealed.** That belongs to Episode 3 ("Aftermath") or later [Source; `Tepenian_Reinterpretation.md` §4].
