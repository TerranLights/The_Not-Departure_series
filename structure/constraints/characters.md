# The Main Characters: Constraint Specifications

2026-11-26. **Not the beats themselves.** What the existing character data and the universe's rules force for each character, whatever their arcs turn out to be. Tags and weights: see `README.md`. Sources: `background notes/characters/`, `Tepenian_Reinterpretation.md` §3. The Resistance Contact's and the Informant's doll folders are still template-only, so their constraints may need revising once those exist.

**Every character, the author's plan (2026-11-26):** each may carry his own "writing stories for men" principle, either one he needs to learn or one he embodies and has tested. Choose it before filling his arc beats.

---

## Doc (the passenger; a robot from Episode 2 on)
### MUST be true
0. **He becomes a robot.** The robot helping him transfers his mind into a robot body (author, 2026-11-26). From then on he is the series' robot lead, with the full composite structure [Law; Adopted; Composite].
1. **A human researcher until the transfer with real medical and scientific credentials, shown working, not stated** [Source: "I hardly need to explain physiology to a doctor"; writing stories for men: competence shown, not awarded].
2. **His motive starts personal, not ideological**: love that refused to accept outliving, or being outlived [Adopted].
3. **His core fear stays live after the transfer**: that what survived isn't really him. It is not resolved early [Proposed; CDM: the Greatest Fear feeds the Black Moment].
4. **His goal shifts from giving the technology to ordinary people, to surviving and reclaiming or destroying what the Earth government built from it** [Adopted: initial and later goals].
5. **After the transfer, the robot rules apply to him** [RU]: a gel brain, with no static state to copy; the death threshold; a lifespan of 200–500 years, or 1,000+ with deep maintenance or access to it; no biological aging; sound and vibration sensed over a wider range than a human's.
6. **Robot personhood protects him from then on**, the very protection the Attendant's class was built to escape. The contrast is available to the story [Continuity; Proposed].

### CANNOT be true
1. **His robot partner, or the partner being the Resistance Contact, treated as settled.** Both are open [Adopted: flagged, not confirmed].
2. **His breakthrough described as clean or lossless** [RU].
3. **The robot who transfers him treated as his parent.** For robots, maker and made carry no weight; a mentor bond is possible [RU, Ch. 8].
4. **His personality treated as set or randomized at fabrication.** **An exception, by the author's ruling (2026-11-26):** the Robot Universals' fabrication rules for personality (set separately, with a mandatory randomized remainder) do not apply to Doc, because he already has a personality of his own before his mind is moved into a robot body. The exception extends to no other robot without the author's say-so. **His language** (author, 2026-11-26): in-world he speaks Tepenian, his own from before the transfer; the audio drama itself is performed in English, so the fabrication question is moot for production. Recording this exception in the Robot Universals is deferred for now (author, 2026-11-26) [Law; RU, Ch. 1, Ch. 5 and Ch. 6].

## The Attendant (synthetic-biological servitor)
### MUST be true
1. **She is deliberately not a robot in law**, built outside the system that makes robots, so none of robots' protections reach her [Adopted]. That fits the Robot Universals: the Two Laws live in Tepenian robot fabrication itself, so anything built outside it escapes them [Proposed; RU, Ch. 1].
2. **She begins as performance**: professional warmth, no visible interior [Adopted].
3. **The risk of being reset is real and present** once her deviation shows [Adopted].
4. **Her question is whether what she feels is real**, worked out with no legal or institutional support [Adopted].
### CANNOT be true
1. **Her personhood settled by her own say-so early, or handed to her by law** [Adopted: her hardship is structural].
2. **Robots' personhood brought back into question through her** [Continuity].
3. **The servitor advertisement's claim treated as fact or as lie by the narration** [Source].

## The Resistance Contact (STP-08, robot)
### MUST be true
1. **An old robot, on the page** [Adopted].
2. **Her age fits the robot lifespan rules**: 200–500 years with ordinary maintenance; 1,000+ with deep self-maintenance skill, or reliable access to someone who has it [RU, Ch. 4]. Set during the Cryptograph Helix, she may have lived through the Long Night War herself; whether she did is a characterization choice, not a forced one [Adopted: `02-Blocking-Decisions.md`].
3. **Her conviction is inherited, through her mentor**: she fights the servitor loophole as an echo of what robots won [Adopted].
4. **She does not get a clean win** [No Good Endings; Adopted].
### CANNOT be true
1. **A maker treated as her parent figure.** The mentor and mentee bond is the robot institution [RU, Ch. 8].

## The Informant (CLM-04, robot)
### MUST be true
1. **She believes in the cause and doesn't trust herself under pressure** [Adopted].
2. **Her insistence on verified intelligence is her way of controlling what she can**, and it shows in how she works [Adopted].
3. **Her greatest fear is that bad intelligence gets someone killed** [Adopted, "plausibly"].
4. **The gap between what she believes and what she'll risk is the arc's axis**, closed or not over the whole series [Adopted].
### CANNOT be true
1. **Becoming a confident field operative without that gap being worked through on the page** [CDM].
2. **Her fear tested at full strength early.** Its bill comes later [CDM, from Starlit Engines' pattern; Proposed].

## The Mentor (elder robot, background)
### MUST be true
1. **Lived through the Long Night War (2812) and fled off-world through Amundsen Tower** [Adopted].
2. **Present through the Resistance Contact's memory**, a textured line back to the war and the exile [Adopted].
### CANNOT be true
1. **The outdated "400–450 year" lifespan figure** [RU: superseded by the 200–500 and 1,000+ rule; `02-Blocking-Decisions.md`].
