# What this series takes from The Cryptograph Helix (2026-10-30)

**Analysis only. Nothing here is decided.** Suggestions are marked PROPOSED.

**The author's ruling on the Helix (2026-10-30):** the Helix was conceived before the Tepenian universe. **Its fundamental structure stays:**
- the DNA mafias spanning Mars, the Belt and Jupiter;
- a resistance that opposes them;
- an Earth-based government, which the resistance also fights.

**What changes or gets scrapped** is the specific factions and characters currently listed.

**The author's aim for this series:** stay as close and true to the original *Departure* as possible, while staying consistent with the Tepenian universe.

## 2. The strongest link: the Elite's own immortality
- **In the original,** the intruder says the breakthrough was hidden away and "saved... for their precious Elite."
- **In the Helix,** the Earth government's leadership are already android mind-uploads, "a crude attempt at immortality." The Helix's technology file treats uploading as "copy-paste," not "cut-paste."
- **Together they fit almost seamlessly:** Doc's method would be the better, rarer transfer that the Elite seize and keep for themselves, the upgrade to their own crude immortality. That keeps the original's line true word for word, and ties the series to the Helix through a *structural* idea, not a named faction.
- **One caution, for the mechanics session:** Robot Universals rules out any clean upload. The Helix's "copy-paste" framing and Doc's breakthrough both have to live inside that rule. This belongs to item 5, the transfer mechanics, worked out with the author in the 05:00-14:59 window.

## 3. Open questions for the author
1. **Is "the Elite's leaders are android mind-uploads" structural** (it stays) or a specific detail (it may change)?
2. **Is this series' resistance the novels' resistance,** or a separate movement? One possibility: an allied or splinter group whose cause is the synthetic servitor class (the Attendant's people) and the robots, against the same Earth government.
3. **Timing:** key the series to *events* ("while the Earth government still holds power, before its fall") rather than to book numbers?

## 4. Inconsistencies inside the Helix docs that touch this series
- **Mars:** `book-spec-sheets.md` (Book 1) has the Earth government in "control over the people of Mars". This repo's `02-Blocking-Decisions.md` cites Helix canon as saying Mars is mafia and resistance territory that Earthtech surveils and assaults, not owns. Both may be true at different points in the series (control early, contested later); worth confirming.
- **Saturn:** Helix Book 5 has established mining bases at Saturn (Titan and others); the master timeline's lore constraints have Saturn at "rudimentary exploration" during the Helix.

## 5. PROPOSED approach, so later Helix changes cost little

  - "the resistance";
  - "the mafias", never a named national mafia.
  - Keep a one-line name map at the top of `Tepenian_Reinterpretation.md`, so a Helix rename is a single edit.
- **Key the timing to events,** not book numbers.
- **Keep the single TANA cross-reference,** which is already safe.
- **Build the series' link to the Helix on the Elite's immortality (§2),** which rests on structure, not on named factions.
